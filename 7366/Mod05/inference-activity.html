Where Did the Inference Happen?

You're about to code a small set of interview excerpts, count the results, and state a finding. It takes about ten minutes. There are no points and no wrong path through it.

The data come from a study of women describing their lives after breast cancer treatment. Your job is the ordinary work of qualitative content analysis: read each excerpt, decide which coping category it belongs to, and let the tally add up.

The point

The leap from these words to this means coping-through-X happened right here, in this one decision — not in the chart. By the time you reached the tally, every interpretive act was already behind you. The count didn't infer anything. It added up inferences you had already made, one click at a time.

Step 5 · The point, named

Krippendorff saw this coming

Writing about a study built exactly like this one — patients interviewed about coping after treatment — he noted that the real interpretive work was done while coding, and that the frequencies that followed did nothing more than summarize judgments already made.

Krippendorff · Conceptual Foundation

The qualitative inferences were made during coding. The frequencies only summarized them.

And the sharper corollary: rerunning the count can only tell you whether your tally is reliable. It can never tell you whether the inference is right. You and the second coder could both recount to the same number and still have confirmed nothing about what that number means.

Where this goes next

Every code you assigned rested on something

When you placed an excerpt, you were answering a quiet question: what is this person's account about, and what am I trying to learn from it? The two of you diverged because you were, without saying so, reading toward slightly different questions. Change that question and the codes move with it.

That quiet question has a name in Krippendorff's framework: the context and the research question. They were governing your coding the whole time, even when they stayed unspoken.

In the next activity you'll make that machinery visible. One text, three research questions, three contexts — and three findings that are all valid and all different.
